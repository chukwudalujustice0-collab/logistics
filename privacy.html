Privacy Policy

Ceetify Logistics — Powered by Ceetify Multi-Ventures Ltd

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Ceetify Logistics collects, uses, stores, and protects information when customers, riders, admins, or visitors use our delivery platform.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect your name, phone number, email address, pickup address, drop-off address, receiver details, package description, order history, tracking information, notification tokens, and device or browser details.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to create delivery orders, assign riders, calculate delivery routes or pricing, send order updates, manage customer support, prevent fraud, improve our service, and maintain delivery records.

4. Notifications

Ceetify Logistics may send in-app notifications, push notifications, email messages, SMS alerts, or phone updates about order creation, rider assignment, pickup confirmation, transit updates, delivery, cancellations, and support issues.

5. Rider and Order Information

When an order is accepted or assigned, customers may receive assigned rider details such as rider name, phone number, ride number, and order tracking information. This is used only for delivery coordination.

6. Data Sharing

We only share delivery information with authorized parties needed to complete the delivery, including Ceetify admins, assigned riders, support staff, and service providers used for notifications or system operations.

7. Payment Information

If payment features are enabled, payment records may be stored for order confirmation, profit tracking, rider settlement, refunds, and business reporting. Sensitive payment processing may be handled by trusted payment providers.

8. Security

We use authentication, role-based access control, protected pages, and admin permissions to reduce unauthorized access. Users should keep their login details safe and report suspicious activity quickly.

9. Data Retention

Delivery records, notifications, support records, payment records, and audit logs may be stored for business, customer support, dispute handling, reporting, and security purposes.

10. Cookies and Tracking

Our public landing page may use tracking tools such as Meta Pixel for advertising performance, audience measurement, and campaign improvement.

11. Your Rights

You may contact Ceetify Logistics to request correction of your account details, delivery information review, or support regarding your data.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our platform grows. Continued use of Ceetify Logistics means you accept the updated policy.
