Company Policy

Ceetify Logistics — Powered by Ceetify Multi-Ventures Ltd

1. Service Model

Ceetify Logistics operates an admin-controlled delivery system. All customer orders are reviewed, accepted, and assigned by the company before a rider begins delivery.

2. Delivery Coverage

We provide delivery services within Owerri, surrounding areas, nearby towns, and selected interstate routes. Some locations may require manual pricing or special confirmation.

3. Order Processing

After a customer submits an order, the admin team reviews and accepts it. Once accepted, a company rider may be assigned. The customer will receive updates throughout the delivery process.

4. Status Updates

Orders may pass through multiple stages such as: accepted, assigned, at office, picked up, in transit, at delivery terminal, delivered, completed, cancelled, or failed.

5. Rider Policy

Only assigned riders are allowed to handle specific deliveries. Riders may confirm pickup, transit, and delivery status. Admin also has authority to update or override delivery status when needed.

6. Package Policy

Customers must clearly describe package type and size. Ceetify Logistics does not accept illegal, unsafe, prohibited, or dangerous items.

7. Delivery Time

Delivery times may vary depending on location, traffic, weather conditions, and operational availability. Estimated delivery time is not a guaranteed time.

8. Failed Delivery

Deliveries may fail due to wrong address, unavailable receiver, refusal to receive, or unsafe delivery conditions. Additional charges may apply in such cases.

9. Payment Policy

Payment may be prepaid, pay-on-delivery, or manually confirmed. Ceetify Logistics manages payment tracking, rider settlement, and profit reporting through the admin system.

10. Pricing Policy

Pricing may be based on predefined routes, delivery zones, package size, or manual pricing by admin for special cases.

11. Customer Responsibility

Customers must provide correct information and ensure packages are ready for pickup. Delays caused by customers may affect delivery time.

12. Support Policy

Customers may contact Ceetify Logistics for complaints, delivery issues, or support through official contact channels.

13. Policy Updates

Ceetify Logistics may update this policy as the system evolves. Continued use of the service means acceptance of updates.
